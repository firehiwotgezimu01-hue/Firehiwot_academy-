Grade 9–12 Mathematics: Algebra, Equations, Geometry and Statistics

1. Algebraic Expressions

An algebraic expression contains numbers, variables and mathematical operations.

Example: 3x + 5

If x = 2, then 3x + 5 = 3(2) + 5 = 11.

2. Linear Equations

A linear equation can often be written as ax + b = c.

Example: Solve 2x + 4 = 12.

2x = 12 − 4 = 8

x = 8 ÷ 2 = 4

3. Quadratic Equations

A quadratic equation has the form ax² + bx + c = 0, where a ≠ 0.

The quadratic formula is:

x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a

The expression b² − 4ac is called the discriminant.

4. Geometry

Rectangle area: A = length × width

Triangle area: A = ½ × base × height

Circle area: A = πr²

The sum of interior angles of a triangle is 180°.

5. Statistics

The mean is the sum of all values divided by the number of values.

Example: For 2, 4 and 6, the mean is (2 + 4 + 6) ÷ 3 = 4.

The median is the middle value when data are arranged in order.

The mode is the value that appears most often.

Quick Review

  1. Solve: 3x + 6 = 18.
  2. Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 4 cm.
  3. Find the mean of 5, 7 and 9.
  4. What is the sum of a triangle's interior angles?

Answers: 1. x = 4. 2. 20 cm². 3. 7. 4. 180°.
