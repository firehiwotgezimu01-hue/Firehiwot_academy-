🧬 Cell Biology

Firehiwot Academy · Biology Notes

1. What is a cell?

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

2. Main cell structures

  • Cell membrane: Controls movement of substances into and out of the cell.
  • Nucleus: Contains most of the cell's genetic information.
  • Cytoplasm: Where many cellular reactions occur.
  • Mitochondria: Produce most ATP during aerobic respiration.
  • Ribosomes: Make proteins.

3. Plant and animal cells

Plant cells usually have a cell wall, chloroplasts in photosynthetic tissues, and a large central vacuole. Animal cells do not have a cell wall or chloroplasts.

4. Quick review

Question: Which organelle makes proteins?

Answer: Ribosomes.
