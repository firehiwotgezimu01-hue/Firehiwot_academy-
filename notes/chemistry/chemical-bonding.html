⚗️ Chemical Bonding

Firehiwot Academy · Chemistry Notes

1. What is a chemical bond?

A chemical bond is an attractive force that holds atoms or ions together in a substance.

2. Ionic bonding

Ionic bonding involves the transfer of electrons from one atom to another, forming positive and negative ions that attract each other.

Example: Sodium chloride (NaCl).

3. Covalent bonding

Covalent bonding occurs when atoms share one or more pairs of electrons.

Examples: H₂, H₂O and CO₂.

4. Metallic bonding

Metallic bonding is the attraction between positive metal ions and delocalized electrons.

This helps explain why metals conduct electricity.

5. Quick review

Question: What type of bond involves sharing electrons?

Answer: Covalent bond.
