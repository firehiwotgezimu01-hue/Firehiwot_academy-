Grade 9–12 English: Grammar, Vocabulary and Reading

1. Parts of Speech

Noun: Names a person, place, thing or idea. Example: student.

Verb: Shows an action or state. Example: study.

Adjective: Describes a noun. Example: a difficult question.

Adverb: Modifies a verb, adjective or another adverb. Example: carefully.

2. Present Simple Tense

Use the present simple for habits, routines and general truths.

Example: I study every day.

With he, she or it, the main verb usually takes -s or -es.

Example: She studies every day.

3. Past Simple Tense

Use the past simple for completed actions in the past.

Example: They visited the library yesterday.

Regular verbs often end in -ed. Irregular verbs have different forms, such as go → went.

4. Vocabulary

Achieve: To succeed in doing something.

Reliable: Able to be trusted.

Essential: Very important or necessary.

Improve: To make or become better.

5. Reading Comprehension

Mimi studies for one hour each evening. She reviews her notes and answers practice questions. This helps her identify topics that need more attention.

Question: Why does Mimi answer practice questions?

Answer: To identify topics that need more attention.

Quick Review

  1. Identify the verb: "The students read books."
  2. Complete: "She ___ to school every day." (go/goes)
  3. Write the past form of "go".
  4. What does "essential" mean?

Answers: 1. read. 2. goes. 3. went. 4. Very important or necessary.
