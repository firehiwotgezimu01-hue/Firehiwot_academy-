🎓 Scholarship Guide

Learn how to search for scholarships and prepare a strong application.

1. What Is a Scholarship?

A scholarship is financial support for education. Some scholarships cover tuition fees, while others may also help with living costs, travel or books. Each program has its own rules.

2. Prepare Your Documents

  • Academic transcripts or report cards.
  • Passport or identification documents, when required.
  • A personal statement or motivation letter.
  • Recommendation letters, if requested.
  • Proof of English or another language, if required.

Always follow the document checklist on the official scholarship website.

3. How to Apply

  1. Choose a country, university or study program.
  2. Check eligibility requirements and the deadline.
  3. Prepare the required documents carefully.
  4. Complete the official application form.
  5. Review your application before submitting it.
  6. Check your email and application portal for updates.

4. Write a Strong Motivation Letter

Explain your educational goals, why you chose the program, what you have learned, and how the opportunity connects to your future plans.

Use honest examples from your own experience. Do not copy another person's application.

5. Official Scholarship Resources

  • DAAD — Germany
  • EducationUSA — Study in the United States
  • Study in Japan
  • Chevening — UK scholarships

Eligibility and deadlines change. Check the official website for current information and confirm whether the program accepts applicants from Ethiopia.

6. Stay Safe

  • Never assume a scholarship is guaranteed.
  • Be careful with agents who promise awards in exchange for money.
  • Verify application fees and instructions on the official website.
  • Ask a trusted teacher or adult to help review unfamiliar offers.
